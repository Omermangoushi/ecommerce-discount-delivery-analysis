## What this means for the analysis

1. **Money comes from `order_items`.** About 8% of order headers don't match their own line items, so line-level data is the single source of truth for revenue, discount and profit.
2. **Profit is recalculated.** The supplied `profit` column counts sales tax as income. We use `profit_before_tax = gross_sales - discount_amount - product_cost` (shipping is charged to the customer and paid to the carrier, so it nets to zero).
3. **Only Completed orders** are used for the discount and category questions (discounts were wiped on cancelled/returned lines) and for the delivery question (only completed orders have a delivery time and rating).
4. **Blank `return_status` = not returned**, and currency is treated as a label.
